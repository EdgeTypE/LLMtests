I The Detective

Sherlock Holmes is a figure of logic in a fog-bound city — a consulting detective who accepts the cases that baffle Scotland Yard, and who can read a stranger's history from the mud upon his boots, the wear of his sleeve, and the callus of his thumb.

He keeps his razor in the butter-dish and his tobacco in the Persian slipper; he plays the violin at four in the morning, and he holds that the highest function of the mind is to reason from the observed fact to the unobserved truth. His rooms at 221B Baker Street have become the address where the impossible is routinely reduced to the merely improbable.

To Holmes no branch of detective science is more important than the art of tracing footsteps — and no case so small that it cannot be examined with the eye of the expert.

— S. Holmes, Consulting Detective
